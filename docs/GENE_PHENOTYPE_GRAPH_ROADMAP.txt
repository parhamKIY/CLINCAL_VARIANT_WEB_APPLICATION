===============================================================================
1. EXECUTIVE DEFINITION
===============================================================================

The gene-phenotype graph is:

    AN EXPLAINABLE BIOMEDICAL RELATIONSHIP AND EVIDENCE DISCOVERY LAYER

Its primary responsibility is to discover, reconstruct, rank, and expose
evidence-backed direct and indirect relationships among:

- patient-selected positive HPO phenotypes;
- diseases represented by source-namespaced identifiers;
- genes linked to those diseases;
- annotated variants linked to evaluated genes.

The graph exists because a flat direct HPO-to-gene lookup can miss relationships
that are reproducibly visible through validated multi-hop evidence.

Canonical MVP question:

    "No exact patient HPO-to-gene match was found. Does the installed,
     manifest-bound evidence nevertheless support an indirect relationship
     between the patient's phenotype and the gene containing this variant?"

Canonical indirect path:

    patient HPO
      -> HPO ontology/MICA relationship
      -> disease HPO
      -> source disease
      -> gene
      -> annotated variant

Ontology traversal, Information Content, MICA, Resnik, Lin, distance, and
best-match aggregation are reasoning and ranking primitives inside this layer.
They are not the graph's final product.

The primary graph product is structured relationship evidence. Scores and
paths support that evidence:

    relationship = what was found
    paths         = why it is supported
    metrics       = how ontology relatedness was measured/ranked
    provenance    = which versioned local evidence supports it

===============================================================================
2. DEFINITION OF HIDDEN OR INDIRECT RELATIONSHIP
===============================================================================

For this MVP, a hidden or indirect relationship means:

    A relationship that is not visible through the current exact HPO-to-gene
    lookup but is reconstructable through one or more validated,
    provenance-backed relationships in the installed local datasets.

It does not mean:

- de-novo biological discovery;
- a novel association inferred from topology alone;
- a causal claim;
- a hallucinated or predicted edge absent from all approved evidence;
- disease probability;
- patient diagnosis;
- variant pathogenicity;
- treatment relevance.

A graph-supported relationship means:

    According to one specific source-hash-bound dataset manifest and one
    specific algorithm version, one or more reproducible evidence paths connect
    a selected positive patient phenotype to the evaluated gene.

If no evidence-backed path exists, the result is unavailable or insufficient
graph evidence. It is not negative evidence and does not prove that no
biological relationship exists.

The graph must distinguish:

1. Direct supported relationship
   - The exact canonical patient HPO term is directly linked to the evaluated
     gene by the approved local association data.

2. Exact disease-mediated relationship
   - The patient HPO term is a positive phenotype of a source disease and that
     same disease is linked to the evaluated gene, even if the legacy direct
     lookup did not expose the relationship.

3. Ontology-semantic disease-mediated relationship
   - A positive disease HPO term connected to the evaluated gene through a
     source disease is related to the patient HPO through the approved HPO DAG
     reasoning contract.

4. Insufficient/unavailable graph evidence
   - A complete approved path cannot be reconstructed or safely evaluated.

Arbitrary weak similarity must not be labeled as a supported biomedical
relationship merely because two terms share a very broad ontology root.
The minimum ontology-specificity rule remains an explicit scientific decision
to freeze before Stage 6B/6C release.

===============================================================================
3. ARCHITECTURAL RESPONSIBILITIES AND SEPARATION
===============================================================================

3.1 Implemented medical/genomic API layer
-----------------------------------------

The application currently obtains factual external evidence through:

- Ensembl VEP;
- MyVariant.info;
- NCBI ClinVar;
- UCSC GenCC/ClinGen-submitted records.

These adapters provide variant, population, clinical, annotation, and
gene-disease factual evidence according to their existing contracts. They are
isolated in the annotation layer. Raw provider responses do not become the
graph product or the LLM input.

3.2 Biomedical graph layer
--------------------------

The graph provides:

- relationship discovery;
- direct/indirect phenotype-gene evidence;
- HPO ontology reasoning;
- disease-mediated multi-hop reconstruction;
- deterministic relationship/path ranking;
- relational provenance and explicit missingness.

The graph consumes validated local graph datasets and the evaluated gene from
standardized annotation. It must not become another unbounded medical API
client.

3.3 ACMG/AMP layer
------------------

The graph is not the ACMG/AMP rules engine and must not assign:

- PVS1;
- PS*, PM*, PP*, including PP4;
- BA*, BS*, or BP*;
- ACMG evidence strength;
- final pathogenicity classification.

Forbidden shortcut:

    aggregate_score >= threshold => PP4

Intended downstream boundary:

    graph relationship discovery
      -> validated structured phenotype/gene relationship evidence
      -> dedicated ACMG criterion evaluator
      -> criterion applicability decision

The graph must not itself assign PP4. Its validated structured phenotype
relationship evidence may be consumed by a future dedicated PP4 evaluator that
also applies separately approved phenotype specificity, disease, gene,
inheritance, and case-context rules.

No production ACMG engine currently exists in this repository. Stage 6D defines
the graph evidence handoff boundary only; it does not implement ACMG criteria.

3.4 LLM/agent layer
-------------------

The LLM/agent may receive only bounded, validated, sanitized,
provenance-backed relationship evidence through the EvidenceObject boundary.

It may explain:

    "No exact HPO-to-gene association was identified, but a supported indirect
     relationship was reconstructed through source disease X and disease
     phenotype Y."

It must not:

- invent nodes, edges, paths, diseases, HPO terms, genes, or sources;
- reinterpret similarity as disease probability;
- infer causality or pathogenicity from graph topology;
- convert graph evidence into ACMG criteria;
- diagnose or recommend treatment;
- receive a raw graph, raw graph database, raw ontology stanza, raw HPOA row,
  arbitrary source file, or unrestricted provider response.

Existing prompt provenance validation must be extended to graph identifiers
before graph evidence becomes LLM-visible.

3.5 Actual system architecture
------------------------------

The implemented project does not use RAG as its primary medical evidence
source. The intended architecture is:

    filtered VCF / manual variant + selected HPO terms

Medical API, graph, ACMG, and LLM responsibilities must remain separate.

4. SCOPE AND NON-GOALS
===============================================================================

4.1 MVP / Phase I scope
-----------------------

Validated local MVP relationships:

    Phenotype <-> Disease <-> Gene

plus:

    HPO child -> parent ontology relationships

Supported local inputs:

- data/hpo/hp.obo;
- data/hpo/phenotype.hpoa;
- data/hpo/phenotype_to_genes.txt;
- the standardized evaluated gene from the existing annotation result;
- existing direct HPO-to-gene matching as an independent baseline/fallback.

The annotated variant is a query-scoped anchor connected to its evaluated gene.
The complete variant catalog is not stored in the persistent graph cache.

4.2 Potential future extensions
-------------------------------

Only after separate data, license, provenance, performance, and scientific
audits:

- gene-to-pathway relationships;
- gene-to-gene relationships;
- protein-to-protein relationships;
- MONDO or other disease ontology normalization;
- additional curated biomedical relationships;
- additional validated biomedical knowledge sources.

Stage 6A interfaces should permit future typed nodes/edges without implementing
or materializing unsupported sources now.

4.3 Explicit non-goals
----------------------

The MVP graph does not:

- replace exact Stage 6 matching;
- filter, normalize, prioritize, or rank input variants;
- create a general-purpose biomedical graph;
- claim pathway, PPI, or gene-gene evidence;
- fabricate MONDO identifiers;
- query remote graph services at runtime;
- use raw patient free text;
- use genotype, phase, pedigree, segregation, or family evidence;
- infer negative phenotypes;
- assign ACMG criteria or pathogenicity;
- perform diagnosis or treatment recommendation;
- replace current medical/genomic API adapters;
- send raw graph/source data to the LLM.

6. LOGICAL GRAPH MODEL
===============================================================================

6.1 Typed logical concepts
--------------------------

MVP node kinds:

- HPO_TERM
  canonical active HPO ID and bounded public name.
- DISEASE
  source namespace plus source-local disease identifier and bounded name when
  available.
- GENE
  NCBI Gene ID when available plus canonical source symbol.
- ANNOTATED_VARIANT
  query-scoped reference to the current standardized variant; not persisted as
  a graph dataset node.

MVP relationship kinds:

- HPO_IS_A
- DISEASE_HAS_PHENOTYPE
- DISEASE_ASSOCIATED_WITH_GENE
- DIRECT_HPO_ASSOCIATED_WITH_GENE
- VARIANT_ANNOTATED_TO_GENE (query-scoped factual annotation edge)

The internal interfaces may use typed node/edge protocols or discriminated
records so future kinds can be added without rewriting query interfaces.
Unsupported future node/edge kinds must not be instantiated or advertised.

6.2 Logical relationship grouping
---------------------------------

The top-level semantic unit is a phenotype-gene relationship grouped by:

    canonical patient HPO ID
    + evaluated gene identity
    + relationship type

Multiple diseases or paths may support that one logical relationship.

Proposed MVP relationship types:

1. direct_hpo_gene
   Exact patient HPO-to-gene evidence from the approved direct association
   source.

2. exact_disease_mediated
   Exact patient HPO -> source disease -> evaluated gene path.

3. ontology_semantic_disease_mediated
   Patient HPO -> approved ontology bridge/MICA -> disease HPO -> source disease
   -> evaluated gene path.

These names are proposed for approval before Stage 6C. Once serialized into
EvidenceObject, changing their semantics or names requires a schema/algorithm
version review.

If both direct and indirect evidence exists, both may be retained as distinct
relationship records. Direct evidence must not erase additional provenance,
and indirect evidence must not downgrade or replace the exact direct result.

7. PHYSICAL GRAPH MODEL AND PROVENANCE
===============================================================================

7.1 Preferred hybrid representation
-----------------------------------

Layer 1 - ontology DAG:

- active canonical HPO nodes;
- child -> parent is_a edges;
- alternate ID -> canonical ID index;
- NetworkX DiGraph only if Stage 6A measurements pass;
- otherwise compact immutable adjacency behind the same query interface.

Layer 2 - compact association indexes:

- gene -> sorted source disease IDs;
- disease -> sorted positive HPO IDs;
- disease -> sorted gene identities where useful;
- gene-ID/symbol identity mapping;
- optional direct HPO -> gene access through the existing loader;
- validated deterministic SQLite or compact immutable records.

Layer 3 - query-scoped explanation:

- only nodes/edges needed for bounded selected relationships and paths;
- never the complete heterogeneous graph;
- may be represented as a small NetworkX subgraph for internal verification;
- only bounded typed evidence is exposed to the pipeline.

7.2 Persistent cache rules
--------------------------

The derived cache must:

- use a non-executable format; no pickle;
- be bound to graph schema version and algorithm version;
- record installed HPO release and all three SHA-256 source hashes;
- record validated node/edge/row counts;
- use deterministic normalized records and indexes;
- be created in a confined private cache directory;
- reject unsafe symlinks and path escape;
- be fully built and validated in a temporary location;
- be atomically published with a completion marker;
- be rejected when stale, partial, corrupt, oversized, or hash-mismatched;
- be invalidated by coordinated HPO data updates;
- never trigger a remote request during scoring.

A non-scoring build timestamp may be recorded for operations, but it must not
affect cache identity, scores, ordering, or golden output.

7.3 Provenance model
--------------------

The manifest is the single source identity record and contains:

- manifest_id derived from stable schema/algorithm/release/source hashes;
- graph_schema_version;
- algorithm_version;
- HPO release;
- source keys, filenames, sizes, and SHA-256 values;
- validated counts;
- cache format version.

Relationship/path evidence references compact source keys from this manifest
instead of duplicating full hashes in every path.

Every serialized relationship must be reconstructable from:

    manifest + relationship record + supporting path records

No raw source row is serialized into EvidenceObject or sent to the LLM.

===============================================================================
8. SCIENTIFIC REASONING CONTRACT
===============================================================================

8.1 Architectural role
----------------------

Semantic reasoning determines how selected patient HPO terms relate to positive
disease HPO terms connected to the evaluated gene. It discovers and ranks
candidate evidence paths. It does not independently establish causality,
pathogenicity, diagnosis, or ACMG applicability.

8.2 Proposed MVP algorithm v1
-----------------------------

The following is the scientifically supported proposal from the feasibility
audit. It must be explicitly approved/frozen before Stage 6B is complete.

Ontology scope:

    is_a relationships among active canonical descendants of HP:0000118
    (Phenotypic abnormality)

Term handling:

- canonicalize valid alternate IDs;
- exclude obsolete terms;
- reject unknown/malformed terms through existing validation;
- exclude non-phenotypic branches from semantic phenotype aggregation.

HPOA semantics:

- include qualifier-empty, aspect P positive disease annotations;
- exclude qualifier NOT from positive evidence;
- exclude non-P aspects from phenotype similarity;
- do not infer patient negation or disease absence.

IC corpus:

    unique source diseases with positive HPOA aspect P annotations

Propagation:

    A disease annotated to term T contributes once to T and every active
    in-scope ancestor of T.

Smoothing and logarithm:

    IC(t) = -ln((disease_count(t) + 1) / (total_diseases + 1))

MICA:

    Common ancestor with maximum IC.

Deterministic MICA tie-break:

1. highest IC;
2. shortest combined child-to-ancestor distance;
3. lexicographically smallest canonical HPO ID.

Ontology distance:

    Minimum directed child-to-ancestor edge count for each term to the selected
    common ancestor; combined distance is the sum of both distances.

Pair metrics:

    Resnik(a,b) = IC(MICA(a,b))
    Lin(a,b)    = 2 * IC(MICA(a,b)) / (IC(a) + IC(b))

If the Lin denominator is zero, Lin is 0.0 for a completed pair evaluation.
Unavailable input or an unevaluable pair is null/unavailable, not 0.0.

Aggregate metric:

- Retain an optional patient-centered best-match average.
- For each patient HPO term, take the maximum Lin value over positive disease
  HPO terms connected to the evaluated gene.
- Average these per-patient-term maxima.
- A patient term with a completed search but no accepted related disease term
  contributes 0.0.
- A gene/query that could not be evaluated has aggregate_score = null.
- Round once at the final boundary using the approved precision.

Why retain aggregate_score:

- It is useful for deterministic relationship/query ranking and bounded output.
- It summarizes patient-wide ontology relatedness.
- It is supporting metadata, not the primary result.
- It is not disease probability, pathogenicity probability, clinical priority,
  or an ACMG threshold.

Relationship acceptance:

- Direct and exact disease-mediated paths are supported by exact source links.
- Ontology-semantic paths require a reproducible disease/gene evidence path and
  an approved ontology-specificity rule.
- Proposed conservative minimum: MICA must be an in-scope descendant of
  HP:0000118 and must not be HP:0000118 itself.
- No numeric Lin threshold is proposed for clinical meaning.
- Whether the proposed minimum is scientifically sufficient remains an explicit
  approval item before ontology-semantic relationships are labeled supported.

8.3 Versioning rule
-------------------

Changes to branch scope, annotation semantics, IC corpus, counting,
propagation, smoothing, logarithm, MICA, distance, Resnik, Lin, aggregate
scoring, relationship acceptance, rounding, ranking, or truncation require:

- scientific decision-log update;
- algorithm-version change when output meaning changes;
- updated golden tests;
- targeted human review.

9. RELATIONSHIP-EVIDENCE AND SUPPORTING-PATH MODEL
===============================================================================

9.1 Internal primary evidence concept
-------------------------------------

GenePhenotypeRelationshipEvidence is the primary semantic object.

Proposed smallest robust fields:

    {
      patient_hpo_id: string,
      relationship_type: enum,
      directness: "direct" | "indirect",
      evidence_status: "supported" | "partial",
      best_lin_similarity: float | null,
      best_resnik_similarity: float | null,
      minimum_ontology_distance: int | null,
      supporting_disease_ids: [string],
      supporting_disease_hpo_ids: [string],
      source_keys: [string],
      supporting_paths: [SupportingRelationshipPath],
      warnings: [string]
    }

The evaluated gene is stored once in the containing phenotype_graph object to
avoid duplication. Every relationship applies to that gene.

Only supported or partial evidence relationships are serialized. Absence of a
relationship record is interpreted through the top-level status, not as
negative evidence.

9.2 Supporting path concept
---------------------------

SupportingRelationshipPath explains why a relationship is present:

    {
      path_type: enum,
      patient_hpo_id: string,
      disease_hpo_id: string | null,
      mica_hpo_id: string | null,
      disease_id: string | null,
      lin_similarity: float | null,
      resnik_similarity: float | null,
      ontology_distance: int | null,
      source_keys: [string]
    }

Gene identity is inherited from the containing phenotype_graph object.
The current variant/gene link is inherited from the containing EvidenceObject.

Paths are supporting evidence, not top-level conclusions. Multiple paths may
support one relationship.

9.3 Deterministic grouping and ranking
--------------------------------------

Grouping key:

    patient_hpo_id + evaluated_gene_identity + relationship_type

Relationship ranking:

1. direct before indirect;
2. evidence_status supported before partial;
3. highest best Lin when available;
4. highest best Resnik when available;
5. shortest ontology distance when available;
6. canonical patient HPO ID;
7. relationship type.

Path ranking:

1. exact path before ontology-mediated path;
2. highest Lin when available;
3. highest Resnik when available;
4. shortest ontology distance;
5. disease ID;
6. disease HPO ID;
7. MICA HPO ID.

Stable null ordering must be specified in code and tests.

9.4 Proposed output bounds
--------------------------

Per evaluated variant/gene:

- maximum 5 relationship records;
- maximum 3 supporting paths per relationship;
- maximum 5 supporting disease IDs per relationship;
- maximum 5 disease HPO IDs per relationship;
- maximum 5 relationship warnings total after deduplication;
- existing maximum 50 patient HPO terms;
- maximum 500,000 pair comparisons per annotation;
- existing maximum 5 source variant rows.

These initial bounds preserve explanation utility while protecting the 64 KiB
EvidenceObject limit. Stage 6D must measure real serialized fixtures and lower
bounds if necessary. Increasing bounds beyond the approved evidence-size and
performance contracts is not automatic.

10. PROPOSED EVIDENCEOBJECT EVOLUTION
===============================================================================

10.1 Top-level graph evidence
-----------------------------

The previous graph_score-plus-paths design is replaced by a
relationship-first structure:

    phenotype_graph: {
      schema_version: "1.0",
      status:
        "success" | "partial" | "fallback" |
        "unavailable" | "not_applicable",
      algorithm_version: string | null,
      evaluated_gene: {
        ncbi_gene_id: string | null,
        symbol: string
      },
      aggregate_score: float | null,
      relationships: [GenePhenotypeRelationshipEvidence],
      dataset: {
        manifest_id: string,
        hpo_release: string
      } | null,
      warnings: [string]
    }

relationships[] is the primary evidence.
aggregate_score is optional supporting ranking metadata.

10.2 Status semantics
---------------------

- success:
  Graph evaluation completed within bounds. relationships may be non-empty or
  empty. An empty completed result is insufficient graph evidence, not proof of
  no biological relationship.
- partial:
  At least one valid relationship/path was retained, but some approved
  data/query work was skipped or unavailable.
- fallback:
  Graph reasoning could not safely complete; canonical direct Stage 6 output
  remains available.
- unavailable:
  Required graph dataset/manifest is missing, stale, corrupt, or unsupported.
- not_applicable:
  No evaluated gene or no patient phenotype input permits graph evaluation.

aggregate_score:

- float only when the versioned aggregate algorithm completed;
- 0.0 only for a completed evaluation whose defined aggregate is zero;
- null for unavailable, not applicable, or unevaluable scoring.

10.3 EvidenceObject version strategy
------------------------------------

Proposed Stage 6D migration:

1. Continue validating and reading EvidenceObject 1.0 exactly as implemented.
2. Introduce additive EvidenceObject 1.1 with optional/required-by-1.1
   phenotype_graph as specified by the final schema.
3. Preserve every 1.0 field name and meaning.
4. Produce 1.1 for new graph-enabled analyses.
5. Read mixed 1.0/1.1 records from the existing database.
6. Never rewrite stored 1.0 evidence merely because 1.1 exists.
7. Keep the complete sanitized object <= 64 KiB.
8. Bump the prompt contract when graph evidence becomes LLM-visible.
9. Extend identifier/source provenance validation.
10. Keep legacy report rendering and current frontend behavior working when
    phenotype_graph is absent.

This schema is a planning contract. Exact TypedDict optionality and validator
mechanics are Stage 6D engineering decisions provided they preserve the
semantic and compatibility rules above.

10.4 Downstream ACMG handoff
----------------------------

Stage 6D must expose a validated read-only phenotype relationship evidence
contract that a future ACMG evaluator can consume without importing graph
internals or opening graph/cache files.

The handoff contains evidence only. It contains no PP4 result, no criterion
strength, and no classification. A future ACMG module owns those decisions.

10.5 Downstream LLM handoff
---------------------------

The sanitized EvidenceObject may include bounded phenotype_graph evidence.
Prompt construction must:

===============================================================================
11. MISSINGNESS, FALLBACK, AND FAILURE ISOLATION
===============================================================================

11.1 Direct Stage 6 preservation
--------------------------------

The canonical direct calculation is independent and must remain callable without
graph initialization.

Valid combined example:

    phenotype_score = 0.0
    matched_hpo_terms = []
    phenotype_graph.status = success
    phenotype_graph.relationships = [
      ontology_semantic_disease_mediated relationship
    ]

This means no exact direct match was found, but a reproducible indirect evidence
path was found. It does not mean pathogenicity or diagnosis.

11.2 Per-annotation flow
------------------------

1. Normalize selected HPO terms through the existing boundary.
2. Compute canonical direct Stage 6 fields.
3. Attempt graph relationship discovery for the evaluated gene.
4. Group and rank valid relationships/paths.
5. If an individual node/edge/path is malformed, quarantine it and retain valid
   evidence as partial.
6. If the graph cannot complete, return fallback/unavailable graph status while
   preserving direct fields.
7. If no gene is present, return not_applicable.

11.3 Whole-dataset failures
---------------------------

Missing, stale, mismatched, corrupt, cyclic, or oversized cache:

    reject cache -> direct fallback -> safe bounded warning

Operation or comparison budget exceeded:

    stop graph work for annotation -> direct fallback/partial

Unexpected graph exception:

    fixed safe graph error code -> no exception/path leakage -> direct fallback

Privacy or evidence-contract violation:

    fail closed at the security boundary; fallback must never bypass validation

11.4 Conservative missingness rule
----------------------------------

Missing graph evidence means:

    unknown / unavailable / insufficient

It never means:

- negative association;
- no biological relationship;
- benign evidence;
- pathogenic evidence against a variant;
- a zero score unless the defined evaluation actually completed.
